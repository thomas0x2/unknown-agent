[public] **EURI** is a stablecoin pegged to the euro, issued somewhere overseas by a foundation nobody in [[The city]] has heard of. It is not DOLR (see [[Money and DOLR]]): no landlord, shop or client in town takes it, and [[Coinbin]] doesn't list it.

[public] EURI turns up in [[The player]]'s life only when [[agent]] gets a price wrong, for example a menu priced in EURI instead of DOLR.

## Writer notes

- In code it exists only as the `prices_euri` menu bug of the restaurant app (`app/content/apps/restaurant.json`, `app/src/engine/restaurant.ts`): the menu shows its prices in EURI. Not a token in Coinbin or Sherwood.
- Was called CURO until 2026-10-17 (renamed by Thomas).
