[public] The Progress Party is [[Daniel Keller]]'s party. Its logo is a donkey redrawn as a mule: taller ears, mid-kick, navy with three white stars and a gold eye. It appears on keller4governor.org and on Keller's ads.

- [public] Keller, a state senator for twelve years, is its candidate for governor (see [[The election]]).
- [public] keller4governor.org, "A state that answers its phone.": affordable housing, buses that run and a state government staffed by people. It promises to cap rent increases in buildings owned by companies with more than 500 units, and a state hiring office for residents who lost work to automation. Every speech Keller has given since 2016 is published there. Fine print: "Treat any video of Daniel Keller that isn't on this page with care."
- [public] Its banner ad: "“I never said that.” — Daniel Keller. Probably." (CTA "Watch the clip").
