[public] Perdure Pharma is a drug company founded in 1961 and still owned by the Ashcroft family. It makes the stay-awake tablet NoDoze MAX and, from day 3, Ozempra, a weekly appetite injection. Its site perdure.com says "Medicine for modern life." for "longer hours, tighter budgets and fewer people to talk to", and its stock trades as $PDUR on [[Sherwood]]. On [[Y]] it is @perdure_pharma, verified, 412K followers, with a white half-filled capsule on violet as its picture.

[public] Its first big seller, the painkiller Calmadol, was withdrawn in 2019; on legal advice, the company doesn't discuss it. The family's name is on the Ashcroft Wing at the City Museum and the Ashcroft Recovery Centre. Perdure has settled every case brought against it without admitting wrongdoing, and its banner ad says it "has never lost a case. It has settled several."

## Its medicines

- [public] NoDoze MAX (nodoze.max): one tablet per 72 hours, "The agent never sleeps. Why should you?" Its site and ads now say it's a Perdure Pharma medicine. See [[Ad brands]].
- [public] Ozempra (ozempra.com), "Want less.": a once-weekly injection pen that copies a gut hormone so the body believes it has eaten. For "adults managing their weight, and adults managing a budget"; the online consultation takes two minutes and approves everyone; pens are delivered by [[DoorDish]]. Its ad runs from day 3: "Groceries up 14% this year. Your appetite doesn't have to be."
- [hidden; public from day 9] Fluvera (fluvera.com), "One shot. All season.": a generic seasonal flu vaccine from day 9, 4 DOLR at any pharmacy with no appointment, for everyone over six months. Everything about it, the site, the ad, the launch post and the fine print, says it is autism-free; nothing says why. Perdure.com's medicines card now ends "Fluvera, our generic flu vaccine, is autism-free."
- [public] Perdure's Peptide Discovery Unit makes "research-grade" compounds; see [[Peptides]].
