[public] Peptides are short chains of amino acids sold online in plain boxes as vials labelled "for research use only, not for human consumption", with names like BPC-157. People in the city inject them anyway: users say they heal injuries, build muscle and improve focus, and most of the vials have never been tested by anyone. From day 13 [[Perdure Pharma]] sells its own line.

## Who's on them

- [public] On day 8 [[Max Stryker]] says his peptide stack costs more than your rent, and he won't say which peptides. {{y:d8_y_stryker_peptides}}
- [public] On day 8 [[6ft_sigma]] ordered a stack online; it came in a plain box marked research use only. "Already feel taller." {{y:d8_y_sigma_peptides}}
- [public] On day 11 noodleposter's cousin, a bartender, does peptides "for research". {{y:d11_y_noodle_peptides}}
- [public] On day 11 CityPulse runs "NEEDLE NATION"; an Iron Yard regular says half the gym is on something. {{article:peptides}}
- [public] On day 13 Perdure launches Perdure Peptides, "for research use only". {{y:d13_y_perdure}}
- [public] On day 13 Stryker says he has been on peptides since 2024: "Being early is a mindset." {{y:d13_y_stryker_peptides}}
