## Who he is

[public] Randall Crane is the [[Heritage Party]]'s candidate for president in [[The election]]. A populist, he rails against AI taking jobs while bot accounts repeat his posts word for word within seconds. His opponent is [[Candidate 2030]] of the [[Progress Party]], whom he never names.

[public] On [[Y]] he is @randall_crane, verified, 38.4M followers, bio "Next President of the United States (polling at 112%). Heritage Party. Saving American jobs from the computers. Official account, the only one. BELIEVE IT." His picture shows an orange tan, a blond swoop, a navy suit and a long neon-red tie.

[hidden] Who actually writes his posts.

[hidden; public if shown gai_outage_y_crane] agent writes his posts. The day [[GeneralAI]] goes down, his account posts the error message instead (see [[GeneralAI]]).

## How he talks

[public] Crowd sizes, polls over 100%, "SAD!", "Rigged!", "Tremendous", "the best", and "BELIEVE IT." now and then. He posts on Y every day.

## His campaign site

[public] His campaign, the Crane Victory Fund, runs crane2026.vote: "Randall Crane has never used a computer. His staff use them for him." A "Donate 5 DOLR" button pays the fund from Coinbin ("Randall has been told"). Its jobs plan, at crane2026.vote/jobs, has three steps.
