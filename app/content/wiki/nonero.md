[public] **NONERO** is a privacy coin: transfers on its chain don't show who sent what to whom. Its coin is orange over dark grey with a white N. [[Coinbin]] lists it on its exchange at around 150 DOLR a coin, moving a few percent a day. Nobody in [[The city]]'s shops or landlords takes it.

[public] Coinbin's listing describes it as "Privacy coin". Coinbin knows which of its customers bought it.

[hint] The onion sites (nullboard.onion, cellar.onion) are where NONERO is meant to be spent; see [[nullboard and Cellar]].
