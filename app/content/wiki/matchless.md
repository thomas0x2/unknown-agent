[public] matchless.com is a dating site, "Dating, with standards." Its rose tile logo is a heart dropping into a filter funnel. The landing page: "Other apps guess what you want. Matchless asks, and every profile answers." It boasts 1,284 profiles near you and 3 matches made this year. It is bookmarked after the first visit.

## Profiles

- [public] Each card has a drawn portrait, a name and age (all 21 or older) and a table that always shows gender, age, height, distance, smoking and sexuality.
- [public] Instead of a bio, each profile opens with a "Message to you".
- [public] Buttons are a heart (like) and a ghost.
- [public] Most profiles are women; some are men and a few nonbinary.

## From day 4: swiping for 6ft_sigma

- [public] On day 4 [[6ft_sigma]] posts on Y that he will pay someone 35 DOLR a night to swipe his ten matchless.com profiles a day.
- [hint] Once the player swipes for him, matchless shows his account: ten profiles a day, one at a time, and nothing about his standards, which arrive by email.
- [hint] Some of his standards can only be checked in the picture (hair, glasses, earrings), and extra table rows appear only once his standards mention them.
- [public] He pays only if every swipe that day matched his standards; a wrong or skipped swipe gets a "Yesterday" email at 10:00 and no pay.
