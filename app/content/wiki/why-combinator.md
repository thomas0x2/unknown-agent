[public] Why Combinator is a startup accelerator in San Fortuna, at whycombinator.com. Its logo is an orange square with a white question mark, and its motto is "Make something agents want." It funds two batches a year; Batch F30 has 412 companies and 9 humans.

## The website

- [public] The front page has the batch counter and an Apply button. Applying is free.
- [public] Requests for Startups lists ideas it wants to fund, starting with "AI agents that replace the founders we fund."
- [public] The deal is the same for every company: 500K DOLR for 7%, plus a hoodie.
- [public] The Companies page lists the F30 batch. WorkAgent is not on it.
- [public] Startup School is free, online and self-paced, with 1.2 million alumni. It is not Why Combinator, and completing it is not an investment. [[Logan Pryce]] is listed among the recent alumni.

## Applying

- [public] An application gets an email from Why Combinator a few minutes later: its application reviewer read every one of the 61,400 applications, and it won't be moving forward. Every application gets the same answer.
