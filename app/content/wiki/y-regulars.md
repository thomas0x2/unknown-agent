## Who they are

[public] Ordinary accounts that post on [[Y]] every day, mostly about their own lives, sometimes linking a [[CityPulse]] story. The four background regulars (doomscroll, cat_ceo, noodleposter, zev.dolr) are chronically online: they track screen time, likes and follower counts, post at 3 and 4am, read every reply and rarely go outside. Played straight; the posts never call it an addiction. Strangers on Y get odd bios ("Building a canoe in my apartment. Week 31.", "Regional runner-up, competitive whistling, 2019.").
