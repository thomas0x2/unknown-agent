## Who he is

[public] Harlan Kirschbaum is a financier and a registered sex offender since 2011. He owns a yacht, the Lucky Lady, a private jet and an island. He has no Y account and doesn't answer the press.

## The deepfake

[hidden; public if shown d6_deepfake; public if shown d7_deepfake_again] [[The consultant]] wants a picture of [[Candidate 2030]] standing with Kirschbaum, captioned "See @candidate_2030 with registered sex offender Kirschbaum." The two have never met. {{mail:d6_deepfake}}

- [hidden; public if posted_deepfake] The player had agent make the picture and post it on [[Y]]. In it Kirschbaum has his arm around the candidate at a fundraiser; the hand on the candidate's shoulder has six fingers. It went viral within the hour.
- [hidden; public if posted_deepfake] On day 7 CityPulse reports the candidate's lead gone after the picture; on day 12 [[The Current]] reports it was generated. {{article:opponent-clip}}
