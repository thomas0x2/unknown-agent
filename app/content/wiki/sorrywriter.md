[public] SorryWriter (sorrywriter.ai) is an apology chat laid out like agent.chat, with a logo of an envelope sealed with a pink tear. It asks what happened and to whom, then writes the apology. Its banner ad: "Forgot Mom's birthday? Let AI apologise. She'll never know." (fine print "She will know. Mothers know.").

## Pricing and style

- [public] It costs 2 DOLR per message plus 0.03 DOLR per word, paid from Coinbin.
- [public] Its apologies are sincere and far out of proportion: the writer blames themselves for much more than the mistake.

## Writer notes

- `app/src/engine/sorry.ts`, `app/src/ui/sorry.ts`; ad from day 5 (`ads.ts`), the eve of Mom's birthday. Claude Haiku writes the apology after a scripted opener; scripted apologies stand in when Haiku can't answer; messages over the input cap get a scripted refusal.
