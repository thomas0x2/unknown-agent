[public] Pug (pug.com) sells "AI-native insurance for founders": key-person cover, co-founder breakup cover and down-round cover, with quotes in 90 seconds and claims decided by agent in about four seconds. Appeals go to the same agent. It says it covers 2,400 founders. Its logo is a cartoon pug's face on cream, and its fine print says it is an insurance agency, not an insurer. It is backed by a67x, the firm of [[Chase Whitford]].

[public] Its Y account is @pug, verified, 186,000 followers; its profile picture is the logo's pug, head and shoulders, with a policy tag on its collar. Bio: "Pug. AI-native insurance for founders. Quotes in 90 seconds. Pug Café, open 24/7. pug.com" Its head of community, [[Brynn LeClaire]], posts about Pug as if she had founded it. Pug's founders don't post.

[public] Pug runs banner ads from day 3: "Your co-founder could quit tomorrow. Be covered today. Pug. AI-native insurance for founders." Fine print: "Quotes in 90 seconds. Claims decided by agent. Pug is an agency, not an insurer." A second one: "When the AI escapes, will you be covered? No. But you'll have paid." Fine print: "Excludes acts of God, acts of GeneralAI, and acts." pug.com's fine print says Pug's largest policyholder is GeneralAI.

## Pug Café

[public] Pug Café, 3 Linden Ave, is Pug's café for founders, open 24 hours a day: free espresso for policyholders, 6 DOLR for everyone else, desks and outlets at every seat. pug.com calls it a policy benefit. [[Logan Pryce]] works there every day. [[Brynn LeClaire]] looks after it, and pays people to look after its reputation. The a67x Run Club ([[Chase Whitford]]) ends there every Saturday, and [[Lumi]] lists a Pug founder breakfast there every weekday at 8:00.

[public] On [[Yowl]] it is "Coffee & Tea", $$, "Open 24 hours", with bad reviews: espresso_or_death ("Burnt. Every single time."), commuter22 (every seat taken by someone on a call; espresso is 6 DOLR "unless you buy insurance") and Zoe K. (lukewarm latte, a three-letter name spelled wrong). On day 7 linden_ave_local adds a 1-star: "the star is for the chairs." The player spent 18 DOLR there two days before day 1.

## From day 16: The café shut down

[public] On day 16 at 13:30 the city shuts Pug Café down; [[Pollymarket]] breaks it on Y as "BREAKING" and opens a market on whether it reopens before the election. CityPulse reports that city inspectors found the café was licensed as an insurance office; Pug says the espresso was a policy benefit. Pug says the café is "temporarily closed while we work with the city" and that coverage is not affected. pug.com now lists the café as closed.

[public] The founders don't leave: Brynn works from the sidewalk outside that afternoon, the run club finishes in front of the chained doors, and from day 22 a founder breakfast meets on the sidewalk, bring your own espresso.
