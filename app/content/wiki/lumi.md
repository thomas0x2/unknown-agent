[public] Lumi (lumi.events) is an event site for founders, investors and everyone around them: dinners, demo days, run clubs. "Find your people. Then find your next event." Hosting is free; guest lists are public by default, "so you can see who's going before you go", and every guest has a profile. Registering means sharing your name, photo and profile with the host and the other guests.

[public] Its featured event is the a67x Run Club (lumi.events/a67x-run), hosted by [[Chase Whitford]]: 74 going, among them Chase Whitford, [[Brynn LeClaire]] and [[Brayden Kolt]]; the full list is visible to registered guests. It also lists a founder breakfast at [[Pug]] every weekday at 8:00, cancelled once the café closes.
