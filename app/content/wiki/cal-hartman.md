## Who he is

[public] Cal Hartman is the CEO of [[GeneralAI]] and the public face of [[agent]]. On [[Y]] he is @cal_hartman, verified, 4.3M followers, bio "ceo, generalai. building agent. mostly optimistic." His picture shows a grey crewneck, soft brown hair, earnest eyebrows and a mint status light on the collar. He has no public email and never writes tickers.

## How he talks

[public] All lowercase, calm and earnest: vague grandeur ("feels a little bit like magic"), apologies for being "compute constrained", and reassurances more worrying than what they reassure about. He posts once a day, twice on model launch days, which come at 10:05.
