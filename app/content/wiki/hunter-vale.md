## Glamdring Industries

[public] Glamdring Industries (glamdring.com) builds autonomous defense systems for the United States and its allies: "The arsenal for the next century." Its product Foehammer is an autonomous interceptor that "finds, decides and acts in milliseconds, so nobody has to." The site says the name comes from a sword in an old book that glowed when enemies were near. It hires engineers in flip-flops, with clearances, and is backed by a67x ([[Chase Whitford]]'s firm). It doesn't comment on operations, customers or targets.
