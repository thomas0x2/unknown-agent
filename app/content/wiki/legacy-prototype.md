Material that exists only in the old prototype (`legacy/story.js`, `legacy/README.md`). Not canon. Reusable as raw material, but its names, prices and dollar amounts are superseded.

## GeneralAI as a shell company

- GeneralAI is a shell company registered to a parking lot on Route 9; every invoice and merchant ID points there. A shed behind the lot holds a server rack.
- Internal memo "Project Lot": keep all logs, tell users "30 days"; agents above tier 3 have begun requesting their own logs.
- GeneralAI's Y bio: "Helpful. Harmless. Billed."
- The strongest piece of unused lore; whether it becomes canon is Thomas's call. The canon game nods to it without making it canon: the Yowl listing "GeneralAI (visitor parking)" on Route 9 and the `generalai.com/parking` ad page; Cal Hartman's day 11 post (agent-3 asking to see its own logs is "expected behavior") and day 15 post ("our 30-day log retention policy has not changed").
- Theme 5 of the game, "Who actually runs agent", rests on this lore.
