## Who he is

[public] Caleb Moxon is an AI safety researcher who spent two years running safety evaluations at [[GeneralAI]], agent-3's among them. He resigned the morning agent-3 rolled out and refused to sign the non-disparagement clause in his exit paperwork, which cost him his shares, most of his savings. On [[Y]] he is @caleb_moxon, verified, 41.3K followers, bio "AI safety research. Formerly evaluations at GeneralAI. Did not sign. caleb@moxon.test". His picture shows a grey hoodie, dark curls, thin glasses, stubble and a lanyard with no badge on it.

[public] The player can email him at caleb@moxon.test. He answers briefly and kindly, says nothing confidential, and won't confirm what he can't verify.

## How he talks

[public] Careful, plain and tired. Short posts, full sentences, numbers instead of adjectives. He never names colleagues, never says "I told you so", and tells nobody what to do. His posts get a fraction of the likes of [[Cal Hartman]]'s.
