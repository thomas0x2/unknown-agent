## Who he is

[public] Daniel Keller is the [[Progress Party]] candidate for governor of the state, on the same ballot as the presidential race in [[The election]]. He has been a state senator for twelve years. His opponent is not named. He has no Y account the player can see.

## His campaign site and platform

[public] His site, keller4governor.org, says "A state that answers its phone." It promises rent caps for owners of 500 or more units and a state hiring office staffed by people, and publishes every speech he has given since 2016. Its fine print warns about videos of him that are not on the page.

[public] The next governor decides whether to renew the Automation Credit, the state tax break a company gets for every job it hands to a machine. It expires in January, [[GeneralAI]] claimed it 11,000 times last year, and Keller says he will let it die.
